# H004 — Falsification

## Refutation condition

H004 is refuted if **second areas** of threshold-blocked major fields measure
a novelty share **above 0.70** in majority. The one-pilot-per-field design is
the known weakness; the second-area test is the refutation instrument
(priority: the borderline fields at 0.67–0.68).

## Borderline cases (measured)

Four fields at 0.67–0.68 (Physiotherapy, Dental practice, Event organization,
Emergency medical response) lie within measurement uncertainty (standard
error ≈ ±0.06 at n = 60) of the 0.70 cut. They must not be counted as
confirmations without the second-area test.

## What would NOT refute it

- Repeating the measurement of the same pilot area: the sample is fixed,
  repetition returns identical values — repetition is not confirmation.
- Quality complaints about the novel atoms: quality is a separate axis and
  not part of this statement.
