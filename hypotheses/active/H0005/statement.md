# H005 — Statement

## Problem Binding

If breadth from model knowledge saturates (H004), genuine novelty must come
from outside the model. Do source-text-derived atoms deliver it?

## Hypothesis Statement

Atoms extracted from **source texts** are semantically more novel relative to
the existing stock than atoms generated from **model knowledge** — measured
strictly **within the same domain**.

## Critical scope restriction

The comparison must be within the same domain: cross-domain comparisons are
confounded. Legal-source atoms already sit farther from the general stock
(median best-neighbour cosine 0.72, range 0.61–0.86 — measured), so a
cross-domain "novelty advantage" of source atoms would prove nothing about
their incremental value.
