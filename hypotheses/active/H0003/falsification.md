# H003 — Falsification Criteria

H003 is falsified if, on small test systems
(1D/2D cellular automata, spin chains with nearest-neighbour coupling):

1. Equilibrium partitions show no locality:
   cells group states independently of interaction neighbourhoods.
2. No equilibrium exists, or equilibria depend only on initial
   partitions and not on the dynamics f.
3. Locality appears only when it is built into the admissible
   partition set (circularity: the result presupposes the structure).
4. Variation of λ leaves partition granularity unchanged.

If falsified:
- Reading "spacetime as interface" in its S-1 form is dropped.
- Record as Known-Failure in the Raumzeit volume (KF-RZ-002).
- BR-24 returns to "open".
